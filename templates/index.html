Interpretable Decision Tree

Uses a tuned scikit-learn Decision Tree with pruned depth to prevent overfitting, making credit decisions transparent and audit-friendly.

🛡️

Bias-Conscious Features

Identifiers (Loan_ID) and demographic attributes (Gender) are strictly excluded from the feature space to prevent algorithmic bias.

📈

Tuned & Validated

Optimized with 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation on the training set, evaluated against a majority-class dummy baseline.

System Architecture & Pipeline Flow

End-to-end workflow from applicant input to probability estimate

1. Data Ingestion

Server-side validation ensures numeric bounds & categorical validity.

2. Preprocessing

Median imputation for numeric features & One-Hot Encoding for categories.

3. Decision Tree Split

Evaluates Gini impurity splits primarily anchored on credit history.

4. Risk Assessment

Outputs probability estimate, approval classification, and rationale.
